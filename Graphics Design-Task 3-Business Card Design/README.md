# Business Card Design

## Project Overview
A professional double-sided business card designed for a fictional UI/UX Designer and the company LUMORA STUDIO.

## Tools Used
- Canva

## Design Specifications
- Size: 85mm × 55mm
- Double-sided design
- Maximum two font families
- 3mm print bleed
- Dark navy and white color palette

## Front Side
- Company logo
- Name: DHIVYA RAJ
- Job Title: UI/UX DESIGNER
- Company: LUMORA STUDIO

## Back Side
- Phone number
- Email address
- Website
- Instagram handle
- LinkedIn handle
